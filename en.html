La Fundação Betânia Onlus

Fundação Betânia Onlus is present in Salvador de Bahia to share life with the little ones and their families, building relationships and accompanying each person on their own path of growth.

Discover Fundação Betânia

Franciscan Fraternity of Betania

At the root of the experience of Fundação Betania ONLUS and the Centro Nossa Senhora Aparecida is the Franciscan Fraternity of Betania.

Through its missionary presence, the sharing of daily life and service to the most vulnerable communities, the Fraternity has for years accompanied a human, educational and spiritual journey that continues to generate welcome, relationship and hope.

Discover the Fraternity

The Nursery School

A place to grow every day

Our nursery school welcomes up to 120 children, from 4 months to 5 years old, offering a safe, educational and completely free environment.

Every day the children receive five meals, care, education and attention. This service also allows families, especially mothers, to work and build a more stable future.

Discover the nursery school

The school project

We are building a school, we are building a future

The school project was created to offer a complete, free and full-time educational journey, accompanying children from 6 to 18 years old in their human, educational and relational growth.
