PAGE GOAL

This page must explain in a simple and emotional way:
how a person, family, company or supporter can concretely help the Centro Nossa Senhora Aparecida and the Fundação Betania ONLUS.

The page must:

* inspire participation
* increase trust
* encourage support
* make helping feel accessible and concrete

---

TONE

* human
* welcoming
* concrete
* warm
* hopeful
* never aggressive
* never corporate

The user should feel:
“Even a small gesture can matter.”
