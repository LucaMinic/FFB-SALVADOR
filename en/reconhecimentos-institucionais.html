These recognitions are a concrete sign of the project's credibility, educational quality and social impact.

A trustworthy educational project

The nursery school and educational projects of the Centro Nossa Senhora Aparecida develop a pedagogical approach committed to Brazilian educational regulations and deeply rooted in the reality and needs of the local area.

Through an education that values childhood, listening, relationships, participation and the many forms of expression, we build meaningful, welcoming and transformative educational experiences.

Partnership with public bodies, educational institutions and civil society organisations strengthens our work, contributing to the continuity of actions, the quality of the services offered and transparency in management.

A bridge between Italy and Brazil

Fundação Betania ONLUS and the Centro Nossa Senhora Aparecida represent a concrete link between Italy and Brazil, built through relationships, educational cooperation and international support.

This dialogue between cultures, institutions and people makes it possible to develop projects capable of generating a real impact on the lives of children and families.

Press coverage and testimonials

Over the years, the mission of the Franciscan Fraternity of Betania and the Centro Nossa Senhora Aparecida has also been told by outside sources that chose to take a closer look at the work carried out alongside the most vulnerable children, families and communities of Salvador de Bahia.

These testimonials offer an outside perspective on our experience and the shared journey that continues every day.

Sky TG2416 set 2022

Salvador de Bahia: the first convent of the Franciscan Fraternity of Betania inaugurated

Sky TG24 reported on the inauguration of the Fraternity's first convent in São Cristóvão, one of the poorest neighbourhoods of Salvador, designed by the Rome-based studio Mixtura with sustainable architecture: natural ventilation, photovoltaic panels and rainwater harvesting.

Read the article on Sky TG24
Diocesi di Molfetta

The Bishop's pastoral journey in Brazil: the story of the final days

The Diocese of Molfetta recounted Bishop Domenico's pastoral visit to Salvador de Bahia, which included a meeting with the Franciscan Fraternity of Betania and a celebration in honour of the founder, Father Pancrazio, recognised as the architect of the community's Brazilian mission.

Read the article
Corriere della Sera16 lug 2024

Mattarella visits the Franciscan Fraternity of Salvador de Bahia, where the meninos de rua are saved

Il Corriere della Sera reported on the visit of the President of the Italian Republic, Sergio Mattarella, to the Franciscan Fraternity of Betania in Salvador de Bahia, highlighting the mission of caring for street children and the community's daily work alongside the most vulnerable families.

Read the article on Corriere della Sera
