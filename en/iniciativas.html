Let's Help Valentina – Salvador de Bahia

Valentina is a one-year-old girl who has attended our nursery school since she was 5 months old. She has two brothers, aged 12 and 10, and a little sister of 7 who has recently finished her time at the creche. Her mother Vania and her father get by doing occasional jobs and live in a small house near the forest, not far from our Fraternity in Salvador de Bahia.
