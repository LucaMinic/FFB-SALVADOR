Distance Sponsorship

A real bond between you and a growing child — education, care and nourishment every day.

Sponsor nowLearn more

A concrete choice of closeness

Distance sponsorship is a direct and personal way of accompanying a child from the nursery school run by the Franciscan Fraternity of Betania in Salvador de Bahia.

With your contribution you help guarantee them education, care and nourishment every day — a real bond between you and a growing child.

How to support

How it works

🤝

Choose to sponsor

Decide to contribute monthly or annually to the journey of a child at the nursery school.

📚

Your contribution reaches the Centre

The funds guarantee education, nutrition, care and support for the child you sponsor.

🌱

A child grows

Your support becomes a real presence every day — a real bond between you and a child.

What you will receive

Photos, updates and communications let you follow the child's journey and see how your support becomes a concrete opportunity.

📷

A photo of the child and their class

📜

A distance sponsorship certificate

📋

An annual report

🔔

Updates on the project

🧾

A tax certificate (on request)

What your support guarantees

🍽️

5 meals a day

Complete, quality nutrition for every child

📖

Daily education

Structured, carefully designed educational journeys

💙

Care and health

Support for the child's health and wellbeing

👨‍👩‍👧

Support for the family

Ongoing support for families in difficulty

How to support

To activate your sponsorship

To activate your sponsorship you need to send an email. You will receive all the instructions on how to proceed.

segretariato.missioni@ffbetania.net

One of our brothers or sisters will contact you to guide you through activating the sponsorship.

"Every sponsored child is a life journey that becomes possible — education, care and a real presence every day."

Your distance sponsorship is not just a donation — it is a relationship, a commitment, a presence that accompanies a child's growth.

Do you have questions?

For any information about distance sponsorship, you can contact us directly.
