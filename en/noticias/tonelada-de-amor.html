Tonelada de Amor

The Colégio Antônio Vieira turned solidarity into more than a ton of food for the families supported by the Centro Nossa Senhora Aparecida.

Share:

The Centro Nossa Senhora received more than a ton of food through a solidarity campaign called "Ton of Love", promoted by the Colégio Antônio Vieira.

Our heartfelt thanks to all the volunteer students of the Colégio Antônio Vieira for their wonderful participation in the Ton of Love Campaign!

More than a ton of food was collected, the result of great commitment, dedication and solidarity with those most in need.

Every item of food collected represents more than a donation: it is the time and commitment to doing good for others.

A day of generosity

“May this act of charity continue to grow in every heart, serving as an example of solidarity and fraternity. You made the difference!”
