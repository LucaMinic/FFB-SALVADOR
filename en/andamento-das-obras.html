School project

Construction Progress

Follow the construction of our school step by step. On this page we gather photos, videos and updates directly from the building site to document every phase of the project.

See the updates

The building site diary

Every update tells a step forward in the construction of the school.

In progressPhase 627 August 2026

The school takes shape – progress on the load-bearing structure

The school's load-bearing structure keeps growing: the columns are now visible, giving for the first time a concrete sense of the shapes and spaces of the future building.

CompletedPhase 523 August 2026

Preparing the load-bearing columns

After the foundation pour, work on site continues with the preparation of the load-bearing columns, the vertical structure that will support the entire school building.

CompletedPhase 421 July 2026

Start of the foundation pour

The pouring of the school's foundations has begun: a decisive step that marks the true start of the building's construction.

CompletedPhase 3July 2026

Preparing the foundations

Preparation work for the foundations is under way on the site, a fundamental step ahead of the start of the school's construction.

CompletedPhase 28 June 2026

Site preparation seen from the drone

The aerial footage shows the first site preparation activities and the organisation of the area intended for the future construction of the school.

CompletedPhase 12025

Initial site survey and gathering of materials

The first visits to the site made it possible to gather photographic documentation, analyse the area and plan the preliminary activities needed to start the project.
